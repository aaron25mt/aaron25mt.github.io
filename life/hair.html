Same steps every week

Wash day

  1. Wet. Thoroughly wet the scalp and hair with lukewarm water.
  2. Cleanse. Apply today's shampoo mainly to the scalp. Massage gently with finger pads—not nails—then work the lather through the hair.
  3. Rinse. Rinse thoroughly. Shampoo a second time only when there is clear buildup or the scalp still feels dirty.
  4. Condition. Coat the hair evenly with today's conditioner and follow the time on its label. If detangling is needed, do it gently with your fingers while the conditioner provides slip.
  5. Rinse. Rinse the conditioner out thoroughly.
  6. Leave in. On damp—not dripping—hair, apply SheaMoisture 100% Virgin Coconut Oil Daily Hydration Leave-In Treatment.
  7. Seal. Smooth a small amount of jojoba oil over the hair.
  8. Dry. Blot with a cotton T-shirt or microfiber towel; do not rub. Air-dry, or use low to moderate heat, until the hair and scalp are fully dry.

Care basics

The two things to remember outside wash day.

At night

Use a satin or silk bonnet, scarf, or pillowcase. Do not cover or sleep on soaking-wet hair.

Scalp warning signs

For persistent itching, pain, inflammation, sores, unusual shedding, or sudden breakage, stop irritating products and see a dermatologist.

Wash calendar

The first Sunday is always clarify + deep condition. Every other Sunday is a normal wash.
